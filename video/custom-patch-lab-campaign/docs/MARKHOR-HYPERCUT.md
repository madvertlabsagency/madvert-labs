# Markhor Patches — Hyper-cut Reels campaign

1080×1920 · 30 fps · H.264 + AAC · no voiceover · no instrumental music · 120 BPM edit grid (beat = 15 frames, fast runs 8 frames).
Render with `npm run render:markhorhyper0N:final` → `out/Markhor_HyperCut_Reel_0N_*.mp4`. Edits are in `src/markhor/h1|h3|h4`; the shared design system is `src/markhor/hyper.tsx`.

**Design system (all reels, from the style guide):**
- Headlines are Manrope ExtraBold uppercase, slanted −8° (the bundled Manrope has no italic face). Type is navy on light backgrounds and white on dark footage.
- 1–3 highlight words per headline sit on an angled cyan #00C2FF → royal blue #1068D7 strip.
- Subheads are Manrope Semibold in sentence case.
- Labels are white bold caps on translucent navy.
- CTAs are navy or cyan pills.
- End cards are bright white / light blue #EAF5FF and carry the ORIGINAL light Markhor logo (unaltered), one headline, one CTA and www.markhorpatches.com.

**Sound:** original rhythm made from sound effects only (`scripts/markhor_beat.py`):
- a crisp noise "tish" on every cut;
- mechanical clicks on the 0.25 s runs;
- low noise thuds on downbeats;
- short whooshes, fabric and needle foley;
- in Reel 03, the clips' real machine audio, cut with the picture.

Nothing is tonal or melodic. Short drop-outs come before the hero and CTA hits.
